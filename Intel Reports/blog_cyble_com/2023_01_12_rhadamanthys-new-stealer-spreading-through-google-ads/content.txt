class="wp-block-heading">Evasive Infostealer leveraging Phishing and Spam Campaigns for its Delivery</h2> <p></p> <p>Threat Actors (TAs) are increasingly using spam emails and phishing websites to trick users into downloading malware such as Stealer and Remote Access Trojan (RAT) to infect users’ machines and steal sensitive information.</p> <p>Cyble Research &amp; Intelligence Labs (CRIL) is actively monitoring various stealer malware and publishing blogs about them to inform and educate its readers.</p> <div id="om-wlravxmwms40sunr2q0v-holder"></div>  <p>Recently, we came across a new <a href="https://twitter.com/1ZRR4H/status/1610590795278712832?s=20&amp;t=AA9C4sZ_LFI8FWw3GnmNMw" target="_blank" rel="noopener">strain</a> of malware called “Rhadamanthys Stealer.” This stealer variant is active, and the TA behind the malware stealer is selling this under the Malware as a Service (MaaS) model.</p> <p>Rhadamanthys stealer spreads by using Google <a href="https://www.malware-traffic-analysis.net/2023/01/03/index.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Ads</a> that redirect the user to phishing websites that mimic popular software such as Zoom, AnyDesk, Notepad++, Bluestacks, etc. It can also spread via spam email containing an attachment for delivering the malicious payload.</p> <h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-spam-email">Spam Email</h2> The collected information includes the computer name, username, OS version, RAM, CPU information, HWID, time zone, user and keyboard language, and others.</p> <p>After gathering system details, the malware queries the directories of the installed browsers on the victim’s machine and searches for browser-related files such as browsing history, bookmarks, cookies, auto-fills, login credentials, etc. It targets different browsers such as Brave, Edge, Chrome, Firefox, Opera Software, Sleipnir5, Pale Moon, CocCoc, etc.</p> <h2 class="wp-block-heading"> Crypto Wallets</h2> <p></p> <p>This stealer malware is also designed to target various crypto wallets and collects information from them. While the malware can target a wide range of crypto wallets, the observed stealer samples were found to have specific functionality to target the following crypto wallets:</p> <ul wallet</em></li> <li><em>WalletWasabi</em></li> <li><em>Zap</em></li> <li><em>Zecwallet Lite</em></li> <li><em>Zcash</em></li> </ul> <p>Also, the Rhadamanthys stealer steals data from the following id="h-conclusion">Conclusion</h2> <p></p> <p>Information stealers are malicious software used to gain unauthorized access to corporate networks, which has become a serious concern. <a href="/blog/exposed-rdp-actively-targeted-by-threat-actors-to-deploy-ransomware/" title="Exposed Remote Desktop Protocol actively targeted by Threat Actors to deploy Ransomware" target="_blank" rel="noopener" data-wpil-monitor-id="14455">Threat Actors</a> use various techniques to deploy their malicious payloads into the victim’s system. In this case, we observed that the TAs used spam email and phishing websites to deliver the Rhadamanthys Stealer, designed to steal sensitive information from the victim’s machine. Additionally, it was also noticed that the malware spreads via Google Ads. It is crucial for users to exercise caution when receiving spam emails or to visit <a href="/blog/covert-delivery-of-cobalt-strike-beacon-via-sophos-phishing-website/" title="Covert Delivery of Cobalt Strike Beacon via Sophos Phishing Website" target="_blank" rel="noopener" data-wpil-monitor-id="14463">phishing websites</a> and to verify the source before downloading any applications.</p> <p>Cyble Research and Intelligence Labs will continue monitoring the new malware strains in the wild and update blogs with actionable intelligence to protect users from such notorious attacks.</p> <h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-our-recommendations">Our Recommendations</h2> <p></p> <ul class="wp-block-list"> <li>The initial infection may happen via spam emails or phishing websites, so enterprises should use security products to detect phishing emails and websites.</li> <li>Avoid downloading pirated software from Warez/Torrent websites. The “Hack Tool” present on sites such as YouTube, Torrent sites, etc., contains such malware. </li> <li>Use strong passwords and enforce <a href="/blog/multi-factor-authentication-mfa-is-a-part-of-your-cyber-hygiene/" title="Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is a part of your Cyber Hygiene" target="_blank" rel="noopener" data-wpil-monitor-id="14467">multi-factor authentication</a> wherever possible. </li> <li>Turn on the automatic software update feature on your computer, mobile, and other connected devices. </li> <li>Use a reputed anti-virus and <a href="/blog/the-rise-of-amadey-bot-a-growing-concern-for-internet-security/" title="The Rise of Amadey Bot: A Growing Concern for Internet Security" target="_blank" rel="noopener" data-wpil-monitor-id="14465">internet security</a> software package on your connected devices, including PC, laptop, and mobile. </li> <li>Refrain from opening untrusted links and email attachments without first verifying their authenticity. </li> <li>Educate employees on protecting themselves from threats like phishing/untrusted URLs. </li> <li>Block URLs that could be used to spread the malware, e.g., Torrent/Warez. </li> <li>Monitor the beacon on the network level to block data exfiltration by malware or TAs.</li> </ul> <h2 class="wp-block-heading" Cyble Research &amp; Intelligence Labs (CRIL) is actively monitoring various stealer malware and publishing blogs about them to inform and educate its readers.    Recently, we came across a new strain of malware called \"Rhadamanthys Stealer.\" This stealer variant is active, and the TA behind the malware stealer is selling this under the Malware as a Service (MaaS) model.    Rhadamanthys stealer spreads by using Google Ads that redirect the user to phishing websites that mimic popular software such as Zoom, AnyDesk, Notepad++, Bluestacks, etc. It can also spread via spam email containing an attachment for delivering the malicious payload.
