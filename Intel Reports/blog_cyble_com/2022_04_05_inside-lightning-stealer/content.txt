An info stealer is a type of malware designed specifically to steal data from the victim’s system. This type of malware has emerged as a serious threat as Threat Actors use them to get initial access to corporate networks.</p> <p>Lightning stealer can target 30+ Firefox and Chromium-based browsers and steal crypto wallets, Telegram data, Discord tokens, and Steam user’s data. Unlike other <a href="/blog/massive-youtube-campaign-targeting-over-100-applications-to-deliver-info-stealer/" title="Massive YouTube Campaign Targeting Over 100 Applications to Deliver Info Stealer" target="_blank" rel="noopener" data-wpil-monitor-id="13002">info stealers</a>, Lightning Stealer stores all the stolen data in the  JSON format for exfiltration. </p> <figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-full"><amp-img data-hero width="936" height="499" src="https://cyble.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Figure-1-Lightning-Stealer-CC-Panel.png" alt="Figure 1 Lightning Stealer CC Panel" class="wp-image-10442 amp-wp-enforced-sizes" srcset="https://cyble.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Figure-1-Lightning-Stealer-CC-Panel.png 936w, https://cyble.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Figure-1-Lightning-Stealer-CC-Panel-600x320.png 600w, https://cyble.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Figure-1-Lightning-Stealer-CC-Panel-300x160.png 300w, https://cyble.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Figure-1-Lightning-Stealer-CC-Panel-768x409.png 768w" layout="intrinsic"><amp-img fallback data-hero width="936" height="499" src="https://cyble.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/cropped-Cyble-Threat-Intelligence.png" alt="Figure 1 Lightning Stealer CC Panel" class="wp-image-10442 amp-wp-enforced-sizes" srcset="https://cyble.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/cropped-Cyble-Threat-Intelligence.png 936w, https://cyble.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Figure-1-Lightning-Stealer-CC-Panel-600x320.png 600w, https://cyble.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Figure-1-Lightning-Stealer-CC-Panel-300x160.png 300w, https://cyble.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Figure-1-Lightning-Stealer-CC-Panel-768x409.png 768w" layout="intrinsic"></amp-img></amp-img><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Figure 1: Lightning Stealer C&amp;C  Panel</figcaption></figure> <p></p> <div id="om-wlravxmwms40sunr2q0v-holder"></div>  <p>Lightning Stealer is a . As the information stolen by such malware is sensitive, organizations should follow good <a href="/knowledge-hub/top-5-best-practices-for-vulnerability-intelligence-in-enterprise-security/" title="Top 5 Best Practices for Vulnerability Intelligence in Enterprise Security " target="_blank" rel="noopener" data-wpil-monitor-id="13010">security practices</a>. In the past, Cyble Research Labs has observed data breaches of large organizations because of such threats. We have also witnessed <a class="wpil_keyword_link" href="https://cyble.com/knowledge-hub/what-is-ransomware/" title="ransomware" data-wpil-keyword-link="linked" data-wpil-monitor-id="1075" target="_blank">ransomware</a> groups leveraging Info Stealers to gain initial network access and, eventually, exfiltrating sensitive data. Lightning Stealer is an emerging Info Stealer, and we may see variants of it emerge in the future.</p> <h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-recommendations">Recommendations</h2> <ul class="wp-block-list"> <li>Avoid downloading pirated software from warez/torrent websites. The “Hack Tool” present on sites such as YouTube, torrent sites, etc., mainly contains such malware. </li> <li>Use strong passwords and enforce <a href="/blog/multi-factor-authentication-mfa-is-a-part-of-your-cyber-hygiene/" title="Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is a part of your Cyber Hygiene" target="_blank" rel="noopener" data-wpil-monitor-id="13012">multi-factor authentication</a> wherever possible. </li> <li>Turn on the automatic software update feature on your computer, mobile, and other connected devices. </li> </ul> <ul class="wp-block-list"> <li>Use a reputed anti-virus and <a href="/blog/the-rise-of-amadey-bot-a-growing-concern-for-internet-security/" title="The Rise of Amadey Bot: A Growing Concern for Internet Security" target="_blank" rel="noopener" data-wpil-monitor-id="13008">internet security</a> software package on your connected devices, including PC, laptop, and mobile. </li> <li>Refrain from opening untrusted links and email attachments without first verifying their authenticity. </li> <li>Educate employees in terms of protecting themselves from threats like phishing’s/untrusted URLs. </li> <li>Block URLs that could be used to spread the malware, e.g., Torrent/Warez. </li> <li>Monitor the beacon on the network level to block data exfiltration by malware or TAs. </li>
