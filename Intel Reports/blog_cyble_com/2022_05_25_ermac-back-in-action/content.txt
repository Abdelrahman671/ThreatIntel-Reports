We recommend that our readers follow the best practices given below:   </p> <h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How to prevent malware infection?</strong> </h3> <ul class="wp-block-list"> <li>Download and install software only from official app stores like <a href="https://cyble.com/knowledge-hub/google-dorks-master-advanced-search-hacks/" target="_blank">Google </a>Play Store or the iOS App Store. </li> <li>Use a reputed anti-virus and internet security software package on your connected devices, such as PCs, laptops, and <a href="/blog/a-deep-dive-analysis-of-butewoorse-ransomware-targeting-mobile-devices/" title="A Deep Dive Analysis of Butewoorse Ransomware Targeting Mobile Devices" target="_blank" rel="noopener" data-wpil-monitor-id="13124">mobile devices</a>. </li> <li>Use strong passwords and enforce <a href="/blog/multi-factor-authentication-mfa-is-a-part-of-your-cyber-hygiene/" title="Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is a part of your Cyber Hygiene" target="_blank" rel="noopener" data-wpil-monitor-id="13132">multi-factor authentication</a> wherever possible. </li> <li>Enable biometric security features such as fingerprint or facial recognition for unlocking the mobile device where possible. </li> <li>Be wary of opening any links received via SMS or emails delivered to your phone. </li> <li>Ensure that Google Play Protect is enabled on Android devices. </li> <li>Be careful while enabling any permissions. </li> <li>Keep your devices, operating systems, and applications updated. </li> </ul> <h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How to identify whether you are infected?</strong> </h3> <ul class="wp-block-list"> <li>Regularly check the Mobile/Wi-Fi data usage of applications installed on mobile devices. </li> <li>Keep an eye on the alerts provided by Anti-viruses and Android OS and take necessary actions accordingly. </li> </ul> <h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What to do when you are infected?</strong> </h3> <ul class="wp-block-list"> <li>Disable Wi-Fi/Mobile data and remove SIM card – as in some cases, the malware can re-enable the Mobile Data. </li> <li>Perform a factory reset. </li> <li>Remove the application in case a factory reset is not possible. </li> <li>Take a backup of personal media Files (excluding mobile applications) and perform a device reset. </li> </ul> <h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What to do in case of any fraudulent transaction?</strong> </h3> <ul class="wp-block-list"> <li>In case of a fraudulent transaction, immediately report it to the concerned bank. </li> </ul> <h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What should banks do to protect their customers?</strong> </h3> <ul class="wp-block-list"> <li>Banks and other financial entities
